# Tunisia Accessibility

This context describes the places, access conditions, and community observations that help wheelchair users decide whether a destination or journey may meet their needs.

## Places and access conditions

**Place**:
A venue or destination that people may want to visit and about which accessibility information can be collected.

**Place suggestion**:
A community-submitted place that has not yet been reviewed or corroborated. It is visibly unconfirmed until then.

**Accessibility feature**:
A specific aspect of a place that affects access, such as its entrance, interior path, toilet, or parking.

**Barrier**:
A physical or temporary condition at a place or along a path that may impede wheelchair travel.

**Unknown**:
An access condition for which the app has no useful observation. Missing information does not mean that access is clear.

## Community information

**Contributor**:
A person who uses a pseudonymous public identity to submit or discuss accessibility information.

**Observation**:
A dated, first-hand account of a particular accessibility feature or barrier. An observation may include a note or photo.

**Unverified observation**:
A newly submitted observation that is visible but has not yet been independently corroborated.

**Corroborated observation**:
An observation supported by another contributor's separate, dated experience of the same condition. Corroboration is not an official verification or a guarantee of access.

**Conflicting observations**:
Observations that describe different conditions. Both remain visible with their dates rather than one overwriting the other.

**Helpful vote**:
A signal that an observation is useful. It does not confirm that the contributor experienced the condition.

**“I experienced this too”**:
A contributor's dated account of having encountered the same condition. It can support corroboration; a simple agreement or helpful vote cannot.

**Venue response**:
A statement submitted by someone representing a venue. The statement is separate from visitor observations, and the representative's affiliation must be checked before it is labeled verified.

**Needs recheck**:
A condition whose expected end date has passed, or whose age makes its current accuracy uncertain. It remains visible with that warning until rechecked.

## Journeys

**Route preview**:
A proposed path that combines map information with available accessibility observations. It communicates unknowns and conflicts and is not a promise that the journey is accessible.

**Wheelchair-first**:
The initial product scope prioritizes wheelchair-related place observations and route previews. It does not claim suitability for other access needs until those are supported.
